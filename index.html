About

I'm Nick, the musician and composer behind TriBird Studios. Music has always been a major part of my life, with 20 years of piano training, 15 of violin, and some experience on other instruments. During my studies I won several local piano competitions and trained under the composer and pianist Joseph Fennimore.

I started composing to combine two of my passions: music, and video games and film. I believe music can lift visual media to a new level, creating deeper immersion and emotion for the audience.

Commissions

I write original music for indie games, short films, documentaries, tabletop campaigns, and anything else that needs a soundtrack. Every piece is written specifically for your project.

How it works

  1. Tell me about your project. The genre, mood, any references you like, and where the music will be used.
  2. I send a sketch. A short draft of the main idea, so we agree on a direction early.
  3. We refine it together. We go through revisions until it fits, and then I deliver the final files in whatever format you need.

What I can make

Game soundtracks
Music for towns, dungeons, menus and boss fights, written to loop seamlessly.
Short films and documentaries
Scores written to picture, from a few minutes of underscore to a full soundtrack that follows the story.
Tabletop and D&D music
Music for the key scenes of your campaign, from a busy tavern to the final boss fight.
Chiptune and retro
Original 8-bit tracks, or retro versions of music you already have.
Stingers and short cues
Short pieces for key moments, like victory fanfares, game-over themes and discovery chimes.
