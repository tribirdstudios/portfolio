Commissions

I write music for indie games, tabletop campaigns and anything else that needs a soundtrack. Every piece is written for your project, not pulled from a library.

How it works

  1. Tell me about your world. Genre, mood, references, and where the music will play.
  2. I send a sketch. A short draft of the main idea so we agree on direction early.
  3. We refine it together. Revisions until it fits, then final files delivered in the format your engine or table needs.

What I can make

Game soundtracks
Themes for towns, dungeons, menus and boss fights, written to loop seamlessly so players never hear the seam.
Tabletop and D&D music
Scene-setting pieces for your campaign: a crowded tavern, a storm at sea, the moment the dragon wakes.
Chiptune and retro
Authentic 8-bit sound, either as original tracks or as retro versions of music you already have.
Stingers and short cues
The small moments that make a game feel finished: victory fanfares, game-over themes, discovery chimes.
