I'm going to use this space for the stuff that doesn't fit in an Instagram caption: how a track from the wheels actually gets written, what I'm learning about writing for games, and what I'm working on next.

## Things you can do in a post

- Write in plain text and add **bold** or *italic* with the toolbar.
- Add a [link](https://open.spotify.com/artist/2xtLBoMBWMckBk6G54xV4P) to a track.
- Upload an image and drop it in.
- Paste a Spotify, SoundCloud or YouTube embed straight into the post.
